# Teamwork
Our team’s planning and coordination has developed gradually through the semester. A clear strength has been our combination of structured meetings and informal communication channels such as Messenger. For each release, we begin with a joint planning session where all issues are created and tasks clarified. Since the releases have been relatively small, this initial planning combined with social media communication and one follow-up meeting was often sufficient. In Release 2, however, we also scheduled a mid-point check-in, which helped ensure alignment. What we have learned is that the balance between formal meetings and flexible communication has given us efficiency without unnecessary overhead.

Still, there are areas we could improve. During the last submission, we encountered a technical issue only on the final day, which created avoidable time pressure. The tasks had been distributed fairly and the planning overal was good, but the lack of a clear role for overall oversight meant that such details slipped through. In the future, it might be useful to assign one or two members to monitor progress more closely, or to hold a short “pre-deadline” meeting to ensure everything is "deliver-ready".

A major lesson has been the growing role of GitHub in our teamwork. Initially, we relied more on meetings and chat, but as we get used to it GitHub is becoming more central as a collaboration platform. As the project gows in size we now see more of the practical value of conventions like structured commit messages, which make it easier to track history and maintain clarity. This has motivated us to strengthen our workflow and be more deliberate about documentation and consistency.
